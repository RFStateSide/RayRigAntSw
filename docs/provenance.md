# Asset Provenance

RF StateSide LLC developed the original RayRigAntSw hardware. Original
contributions and retained upstream material have distinct rights; the project
license does not override third-party licenses.

| Included material | Authorship and treatment |
|---|---|
| Hardware design, BOM and engineering documentation | RF StateSide LLC original work and documented modifications; CERN-OHL-P-2.0 |
| Custom SO-239, RCA, capacitor and connection/RF-pad footprints | Personally created by the RF StateSide owner in the MyFootprints and MyConnectors library collection; original contributions covered by the project license |
| Standard KiCad-derived symbols and footprints | KiCad library contributors; applicable CC-BY-SA-4.0 terms with the KiCad library exception |
| Customized library definitions retaining upstream elements | Original modifications and upstream rights remain distinct |
| Schematic and PCB SVGs | KiCad exports of the included design; not photographs or physical test evidence |
| Official legal texts | Original licensors' authorship and wording retained |

## CAD Collection

Required definitions were extracted from the embedded schematic and placed PCB.
Their working pin definitions and geometry were retained instead of refreshing
them from potentially different global libraries.

The owner confirmed personal creation of all custom footprints previously in
question, including SO_239, CUI_RCJ-021, CAPRR500W50L730T250H650, Conn_Tp1 and
Conn_Pad_Tp. Historical descriptions and procurement identifiers in those files
are component-reference metadata, not evidence of a downloaded footprint.

The mixed symbol collection includes standard KiCad families and connector
customizations retained from the project libraries. The footprint authorship
confirmation is not a blanket claim that all embedded symbol definitions are
exclusively original. Applicable KiCad notices remain with the collection,
including the stock diode/terminal and upstream-derived relay definitions.

See [library attribution](../hardware/libraries/README.md). Project-local
library tables use relative paths. No external model binaries are bundled.

## Component References and Images

Manufacturer and supplier fields are historical procurement references. They do
not establish current availability, suitability of substitutes or the exact
parts installed in every tested assembly.

No vendor datasheets, STEP models, supplier order records, website photographs,
AI-enhanced photographs or historical interactive-BOM software are included.
The SVGs are source renderings and follow the underlying design attribution.

Original archives and internal review evidence are retained separately. ChatGPT
and Codex assistance is acknowledged under Development Tools in the README. The
public engineering audit has its own attribution.
